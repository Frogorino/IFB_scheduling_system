Flexible Multi-Format Excel & Dispatch Support

The parser automatically detects headers, merges stacked multi-row parent headers, and maps columns flexibly:

Format 1: Multi-Month Plan

Part details with rolling month-year columns (e.g. Oct-26, Nov-26, Dec-26). First month is Firm, subsequent months are Tentative.

Format 2: Stacked Multi-Month Dispatch Data

Row 1 has merged Month headers (e.g. Apr-26, May-26) spanning Customer Schedule, Company Schedule, and Dispatch QTY sub-columns.
